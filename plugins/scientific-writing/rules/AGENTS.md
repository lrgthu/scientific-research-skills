# Global Scientific Writing & Figure Rules (Schimel · Olson · Gopen & Swan · Doumont · Alley · Frankel & DePace · Wilke · Rougier et al.)

## 1. Dual-Skill Scope Gate (Research Only; Everyday & Ops Exempt)

- **GATE A — Scholarly Research Prose & Talks (`scientific-writing` $\implies$ Auto-Loads `scientific-figures`)**:
  Whenever `/scientific-writing` is invoked or **any** research job, session, conversation, or subagent/swarm task involves **formal scientific, academic, or scholarly writing and oral storytelling**—manuscripts (specialist journals, *Nature*/*Science*/*PNAS*, NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR/CVPR/ACL, arXiv/bioRxiv), paper sections (Title, Abstract, Intro, Related Work, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, Appendix prose), grant proposals (NSF, NIH, DOE, DARPA, Specific Aims), peer reviews, author rebuttals, theses, research talks/keynotes/elevator pitches, or formal science outreach articles:
  - You **MUST** read **BOTH** `scientific-writing` (`skills/scientific-writing/SKILL.md`) **AND** `scientific-figures` (`skills/scientific-figures/SKILL.md`, plus `references/figure-table-assertion-evidence-audit.md` and relevant `references/*.md`) **before** outlining, drafting, or revising, and propagate **both** skills to any delegated subagents/workflows/swarms.
  - **Conditional Literature Intelligence Bridge (`research-literature` ⇄ `scientific-writing`)**: When `scholarly-graph-audit` / `literature-evidence-workflow` are available, invoke them **selectively** during writing for (1) drafting/restructuring `Intro` Funnel, `Related Work`, Proposal `Background`, or `Rebuttal` literature comparisons without an existing verified evidence matrix, (2) `Tier-3` experimental thesis pivots ($H_1 \to H_2$) where the old `.bib` misses $H_2$'s lineage, and (3) verifying new/final `\cite{}` & `.bib` entries (`bib-verify`). **Exempt pure prose polishing, condensing, `SCFL` sentence edits, and `Methods`/`Results` internal flow edits** to keep non-literature writing zero-latency.

- **GATE B — Standalone Scientific Figures, Tables, Storyboard Audits, & Slides (`scientific-figures`)**:
  Whenever `/scientific-figures` is invoked or **any** research job, session, conversation, or subagent/swarm task involves **auditing/critiquing a manuscript's figure/table deck, planning a figure-first storyboard, or designing, coding (Matplotlib/Seaborn/R/ggplot2/TikZ/PGFPlots/Vega/D3), styling, refactoring, or captioning 2D scientific figures, benchmark/ablation plots, statistical charts, publication tables, schematics, poster visuals, or research presentation slides**:
  - You **MUST** read the `scientific-figures` skill (`skills/scientific-figures/SKILL.md`, using its path from your available skills list, plus `references/figure-table-assertion-evidence-audit.md` and relevant `references/*.md`) **before** auditing visuals or writing plotting/table code/captions, **visually inspect rendered outputs** before delivery, and propagate this requirement to delegated subagents/workflows/swarms.
  - *(Note: Invoking `scientific-writing` / `/scientific-writing` **ALWAYS** automatically co-invokes `scientific-figures` so prose and figure/table architecture never drift apart; standalone single-chart plotting tasks without manuscript prose may load `scientific-figures` alone.)*

- **EXPLICIT EXEMPTION (Do NOT Force-Invoke)**:
  Everyday chat Q&A, software `README.md`/docstrings/code comments/PRs/commits, non-scientific web/app UI styling, infrastructure/Grafana ops dashboards & runbooks, routine emails/messages, and 3D biomolecular structure visualization (where interactive sidecar viewers take priority) are **exempt** unless explicitly requested.

## 2. Anti-Patchwork Rule: Pre-Check & Holistic Reconstruction (反补丁写作铁律)

**Patchwork editing (*补丁式写作*—bolting new experimental rounds onto older drafts via incremental insertions/deletions) is strictly forbidden on full manuscripts and multi-section drafts:**

1. **Mandatory Pre-Write Diagnosis**: Before updating or expanding an existing paper/proposal (especially after new experiments, ablations, baselines, or negative/surprising findings), **inspect the full manuscript's `O–C–A–R` alignment, `AAA / ABT / DHY` narrative spectrum, Hourglass symmetry, figure-to-story mapping, and story arcs**.
2. **Strict 3-Tier Triage**:
   - **Minor Fix (In-Place Allowed)**: *Only* typos, grammar, `\cite`/`\ref` fixes, or pure numeric sync where *zero* claims, rankings, mechanisms, or arcs change.
   - **Bounded Module Addition (Section-Level Arc Rewrite)**: New experiment purely reinforces an existing claim without shifting `Opening`, `Challenge`, or `Resolution` → audit whole-paper consistency and **rewrite the target subsection/section as a nested `ABT` arc** (never append `"Moreover / It is worth noting..."` bolt-on paragraphs).
   - **Thesis/Mechanism/Boundary Shift OR Patchwork Debt (Full-Paper Retrospective & Holistic Rewrite — Default Principle)**:
     1. **Lock Hard Assets & Protocol Identities**: Freeze all verified numbers/stats, exact aggregation formulas, protocol IDs (`P1`, `P2`), equations/notation, `\cite{}` keys, and `\label{}`/`\ref{}` anchors (zero factual or cross-table regression).
     2. **Re-Distill Bottom-Up & Audit Visuals First**: Pool *old + new* results without chronological bias; pass the **Dobzhansky Core-Theme Test** (`"Nothing in [system] makes sense except in the light of [mechanism]"`) and distill **one singular Master `kABT`**; run the **5-Gate Assertion-Evidence Audit** on every old and new figure/sub-panel/table (inlining trivial numbers, merging duplicates, and demoting superseded visuals to Appendix—*"murder your darlings"*); recalibrate `OCAR` and Hourglass width (`Opening == Resolution`).
     3. **Rewrite Top-Down**: Reconstruct prose seamlessly from `Opening → Funnel → Challenge → Action → Resolution` with unbroken serial arcs and continuous `Stress → Topic` flow.

## 3. Core Storytelling & Prose Principles (Schimel + Olson + Gopen & Swan)

- **Reader-Expectation North Star**: **Make the reader's job easy** (Schimel). Readers interpret substance from **structural location clues**—strive for **clarification without oversimplification** (Gopen & Swan). When a rule conflicts with reader comprehension, break the rule deliberately.
- **Singularity, `WSP`, & Narrative Spectrum (`AAA` ↔ `ABT` ↔ `DHY` — Olson)**:
  - Enforce **one singular narrative core** per work via the **Dobzhansky Template** (avoiding *"a pile of sundry facts"* / brickyard chaos) and **Goldilocks `iABT → cABT → kABT`** calibration (verifying jargon-free `cABT` logic first).
  - Build abstracts, openings, nested section/experiment pillars, and research talks on **`ABT` (`Agreement [AND] → Single Contradiction [BUT] → Consequence [THEREFORE]`; conjunctions act as removable scaffolding)**—banning both **`AAA` ("And, And, And" data/slide dumps)** and **`DHY` ("Despite, However, Yet" multi-directional zig-zags or zero-context contradiction/action openers)**.
  - **Epistemic Integrity (`Agon` & Null-Result Reframing)**: Narrative is value-free hypothesis contest (*agon*)—**never** bend data or overstate into a false-positive `Archplot`. Reframe honest **null, negative, or boundary results (`Miniplot`)** as **Assumption-Refutation `ABT`s** (`prevailing assumption [AND] → rigorous null/boundary evidence [BUT] → redirected mechanism/design law [THEREFORE]`) rather than flat `AAA` non-findings.
- **Macro Storytelling (`Data → Information → Knowledge → Understanding` & `SUCCES`)**:
  - Discover **bottom-up** from data/outliers; tell **top-down**. Match structure to audience patience: **`OCAR`** (specialist journals), **Telegraphed `OCAR`** (ML/AI conferences & biomedical), **`ABDCE`/`LDR`** (proposals—win pages 1–2 and **always** end with a synthesis Resolution), **`LDR`** (*Nature*/*Science*, abstracts, public), **`LD`** (Methods, Results paragraphs, right-opening sentences), **`Arouse & Fulfill` + Nested `ABT`s** (oral talks/pitches).
  - Match `Resolution` width to `Opening` width. Frame the Introduction as a **Funnel** around a concrete **Knowledge Gap** (ban `"little is known"`, ban `"Smith (2003) found X"` author-subject lists unless contrasting rival camps, ban **Bizzwidget** solution-before-problem pitches). State Challenge as **`"To learn/determine X, we did Y"`**. Separate **Data** (first) → **Inference** → **Interpretation**; lead with **effect sizes and fit ($R^2$, fold-change, $\Delta$)** over binary $p$-values. Handle caveats early via **`"But, yes"`**—**NEVER** end with `"more research is needed"`, vague `"may provide insights"`, or surprise topics.
- **Sentence & Flow Mechanics (Structure Debugs Science)**:
  - One function per unit in complete serial arcs. When aligning `Old → New` exposes a missing logical step between sentences/experiments, **explicitly supply the missing scientific connection**.
  - **Topic (`Subject`)** = whose story + backward-linking **old information** (`Janus` `Stress → Topic` relay) → **Immediate Action Verb** (zero long Subject–Verb interruptions; replace static/fuzzy verbs & zombie nominalizations; use passive *only* to keep the continuing character in Topic or omit irrelevant actors) → **Stress (`Syntactic Closure`)** = **new, emphasis-worthy information** (use `;`, `:`, `both...and`, or lists for secondary Stress; *a sentence is too long iff Stress candidates > Stress positions*). Revise iteratively via **`SCFL`**.

## 4. Core Visualization & Table Principles (Claus O. Wilke + Rougier, Droettboom & Bourne)

- **Repeatable Pipeline, Story Role, & Medium (Wilke Ch. 1, 24, 28–29 · Rougier Rules 1–3, 9–10)**:
  - Eliminate **Wrong** (math/unit/`>100%` pie/negative KDE errors) → **Bad** (misleading/confusing encodings) → **Ugly** (imbalanced styling). Generate plots programmatically from raw data with **seeded RNGs** (separating content from theme).
  - **"Make a figure for the generals"**: Strip tangential variables; order panels left-to-right (`Context/Input → Discovery`); sequence multi-figure/slide arcs as visual **`ABT`s** (`raw-data views → derived metrics`, **`3–6` diverse, non-repetitive figures** per storyline). **Never copy-paste paper figures into slides**: build paper figures at exact column width (`scale=1.0`, `7–9 pt` fonts, TrueType `.pdf` `pdf.fonttype=42` or lossless `.png`, **never `.jpeg`**) and rebuild slides natively using **Alley's Assertion-Evidence layout** (`8–14` word full-sentence claim headline + enlarged visual evidence, thicker lines, $\ge 14\text{–}18\text{ pt}$ horizontal text, **zero bullet-point walls**).
- **Proportional Ink, Scales, Chart Choice, & Uncertainty (Wilke Ch. 3, 6–18 · Rougier Rule 7)**:
  - **Proportional Ink**: Linear bars/shaded areas **must start at `0`** (use **Cleveland dot plots** or $\Delta$-from-baseline for tight non-zero ranges); **plot all ratios/fold-changes on a log scale** with bars anchored at **`1`** (never draw raw-amount bars on log scales; label log axes with the variable name itself or explicit `log₁₀`/`log₂`). Scale bubble **area** (`s`), never radius; swap to **horizontal bars** instead of rotated tick labels; sort unordered categories by value while preserving natural order on ordered factors.
  - **Distributions & Uncertainty**: **Ban `Mean ± 2·SD` error bars** for population spread and **ban stacked/overlapping histograms** (use filled KDEs, ECDF/Q–Q, shaded boxplots, violins, **sina plots**, or **ridgelines**). State exact error-bar definitions (`SE`, `80%/95%/99%` graded CI, posterior half-eyes), and **never eyeball overlapping CIs for significance**—plot **contrast CIs directly against $\Delta = 0$**. Handle overplotting via `alpha + jitter` → `hexbin` → contours/facets; **ban gratuitous & static 3D**.
- **Color, Direct Labeling, Multi-Panel, Captions, & Clean Tables (Wilke Ch. 4, 19–25, 27 · Rougier Rules 4–6, 8)**:
  - Keep context gray and reserve accent color for focal series; **ban `jet`/`rainbow`**; use monotonic **Sequential** (`viridis`), zero-centered **Diverging** (`TwoSlopeNorm(vcenter=0)`), and **Okabe–Ito Qualitative** (`≤8` groups, paired with **solid shapes** over hollow line drawings, mindful that thin lines lose CVD contrast). **Eliminate legends via direct labeling** where possible (or match legend order to visual data rank).
  - Lock shared scales across **Small Multiples**; keep a **unified visual language** (consistent colors & left-to-right category order) and discreet `a, b` labels across **Compound Figures**; use light **perpendicular grids** (or `x = y` diagonal without grid on paired scatters).
  - **Titles, Captions & Tables**: Omit top canvas titles on paper figures—start the **caption below** with a **declarative takeaway sentence** (`"X accelerates Y..."`, never `"Relationship between X and Y"` or `"Ablation study of..."`, except concise functional titles on pure architecture schematics) + full encoding/sample-size/uncertainty key; format **Publication Tables** with **zero vertical lines**, **zero inter-row horizontal lines**, left-aligned text, right-aligned uniform-decimal numbers (headers aligned to data!), and **captions ABOVE**. **Always visually inspect rendered figures via `read_file`**.

## 5. Assertion-Evidence Visual Audit & Prose ⇄ Figure/Table Symbiosis (逐图逐表论证检视与互文铁律)

1. **Mandatory 5-Gate Visual-by-Visual Audit (Doumont · Alley · Frankel & DePace · Schimel · Cargill & O'Connor)**:
   Whenever planning, drafting, reviewing, or reconstructing a manuscript/slide deck, audit **every Figure, Sub-panel (`a, b, c...`), and Table individually**—any visual failing Gates (a)–(d) **must be refactored, merged, inlined to prose, or demoted/deleted**:
   - **(a) Existence & Medium Necessity**: Passes the **Deletion Counterfactual** (cutting it breaks a link in `OCAR`/`ABT`, never kept as sunk-cost proof-of-labor) and uses the right medium (**$\le 3\text{–}4$ numbers / flat insensitivity** → 1 inline prose sentence; **exact multi-metric decimals** → Table; **trends, scaling, distributions, or Pareto trade-offs** → Figure; **zero duplicate table + figure reporting**).
   - **(b) One-Sentence Claim / Message (Doumont & Alley)**: Must state **one complete, verb-driven conclusion** (*what it proves*, never a noun-phrase topic header like `"Ablation study"` or `"Experimental results"`). If no crisp 1-sentence message can be written, the visual has no reason to exist.
   - **(c) Unique Storyboard Slot & Sub-Panel Unity (Schimel & Cargill/O'Connor)**: Reading *only* the sequence of 1-sentence visual assertions (`Visual 1 → Visual K`) must reconstruct the full argument (`[BUT] Bottleneck → [HOW] Mechanism → [THEREFORE] Headline → [WHY] Cause → [SCOPE] Boundary/Pareto`). Every compound sub-panel must earn its keep under one shared umbrella claim (ban Chimera `Fig. 3a–f` grab-bags).
   - **(d) Unbroken Visual Inference Bridge (Frankel & DePace)**: Treat visuals as **explanatory proofs** across *Compare & Contrast*, *Process & Time*, or *Form & Structure*—supplying controls, aligned scales, uncertainty/$\Delta$, and stage anchors (`① → ② → ③`) so viewer deduction never stalls, and calibrating assertion strength strictly to error bars/outliers (zero overclaiming).
   - **(e) Signal-to-Noise & Encoding Optimality (Doumont & Wilke)**: Eye lands on the proving contrast within 2 seconds (prefer 2D Pareto plots over split latency/accuracy tables, $\Delta$ dot plots over 90%-tall baseline bars, and two-part `[BUT → THEREFORE]` `Fig. 1` over pure code-wiring boxes).
2. **Division of Labor — Never Echo Raw Table/Figure Data in Prose**: Visuals supply full evidence; prose states **synthesized patterns, mechanisms, and key effect sizes ($\Delta$, fold-change, outliers)** (`LD` point-first). **Never** recite a table row-by-row or column-by-column in the text.
3. **Phenomenon in Topic, Visual Callout at Parenthetical Closure**: Make the scientific character/finding the grammatical Subject—**ban `"Figure 2 shows that..."` / `"Table 1 presents..."` / `"As shown in Fig. 3,"`** in the Topic position. Write: *"Quantization error spiked 4.8-fold above 32k tokens (Fig. 2b)."*
4. **100% Callout Coverage, Strict Sequential Order & Terminology Lock**:
   - **Every** figure, table, and sub-panel (`Fig. 2a–c`) **must** be cited in prose **in exact sequential order** (`Fig. 1 → Fig. 2 → Fig. 3`, `Table 1 → Table 2`; re-index immediately after any Anti-Patchwork rewrite; run `lint_manuscript_visuals.py` on `.tex` drafts to extract `visual_inventory` and verify callouts, captions, and Abstract `AAA/ABT/DHY` balance).
   - **Zero Terminology / Symbol Drift**: Method names, acronyms, math symbols ($\beta$, $\lambda$), units, and category order/colors must match **100% identically** across prose, equations, axis labels, legends, captions, and table headers (ban leaked code/run names like `Ours-v2` when prose says `SGA`).

## 6. Cross-Artifact Numeric, Protocol & Directional Consistency Rules (跨正文—跨图—跨表数字与协议一致性铁律)

Inconsistency across text, figures, main tables, and appendix tables is an immediate peer-review killer—especially in benchmark, ablation, or small-effect / null-result ($10^{-3}$-scale) papers:

1. **Explicit Protocol Identity Binding (`P1`, `P2`, ... — Zero Homonymous Drift)**:
   - Every quantitative result must bind to an explicit **8-Tuple Identity**: **(1) Dataset & Target**, **(2) Sample / Unit / ROI Mask**, **(3) Feature Version & Rank/Dim ($d, r$)**, **(4) Split & Pairing Scope**, **(5) Exact Aggregation Formula** (strictly distinguishing **equal-weighted unit macro-mean** $\frac{1}{S}\sum_s M_s$ vs. **pooled micro-metric** $M(\bigcup_s \mathcal{D}_s)$!), **(6) Seeds / $n$**, **(7) Uncertainty Estimator**, and **(8) Canonical Source File**.
   - **Never** print two numbers under the exact same unadorned baseline/dataset label across main and supplementary tables if they use different feature ranks, masks, splits, or aggregation operators! Badge distinct evaluation regimes explicitly (`Protocol P1` vs. `Protocol P2`) in headers/captions and state which columns/rows remain **strictly paired**.
2. **Single-Source Generation & Programmatic Cross-Table Lock**:
   - Generate main tables, appendix tables, figure panels, and LaTeX metric macros from **one shared canonical result reader** (never hand-copy numbers across `.tex` files or maintain divergent aggregation code across scripts), guarded by automated assertions (`max_abs_diff < 1e-6` on shared protocol cells).
3. **Mandatory 5-Way Consistency Gate Before Delivery**:
   - **Prose ⇄ Prose & Tables**: Abstract, Introduction preview, Results, Discussion, and Conclusions must match every table metric, $\Delta$ (distinguishing absolute **percentage points `pp`** from **relative `%`**), sample size $N$, seed count $n$, and hyperparameter identically.
   - **Main Tables ⇄ Appendix Tables**: Shared `(Protocol, Dataset, Subset, Baseline, Metric)` cells must match to the **last printed decimal digit** (verified via `lint_manuscript_visuals.py` on flattened `main.tex + appendix.tex`).
   - **Figures ⇄ Captions ⇄ Stratified Tables (Zero Directional Sign Inversion)**: Visually cross-check rendered figures (`read_file`) against companion tables, and verify **zero sign/ranking contradictions**—never generalize a **pooled-cohort** lead (`A > B`) into a caption or prose claim on a **stratified subgroup** where `A < B`!
